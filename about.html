About Me

The person behind the code

A closer look at who I am, what drives me, and how I approach engineering problems.

My Story

I'm a final-year Engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University with a strong interest in the space where hardware meets software, and in design. Alongside my engineering coursework, I work as a web developer and graphic designer, and I program in C/C++ and Python.

Over the course of my degree, I've learned that good engineering is less about memorizing syntax and more about disciplined problem solving: breaking a system into parts, understanding constraints, and iterating until the solution is both correct and elegant.

How I Work

I like documenting as I build, testing early, and keeping designs modular so that individual components can be reasoned about on their own. I'm comfortable working independently on long-form projects like my capstone, and I enjoy collaborative work where different specialties come together.

Outside of formal coursework, I try to keep a small personal project running at all times — it's the fastest way I've found to actually learn a new tool or concept.

Core Values

Curiosity

I default to asking "how does this actually work?" before accepting a black box.

Precision

In engineering, small errors compound — I care about getting the details right the first time.

Continuous Learning

Technology moves fast; I treat every project as a chance to pick up a new skill.

Frequently Asked

What area of Computer Engineering interests you most?

Embedded systems and digital hardware design — specifically the overlap between firmware and the physical sensors/actuators it controls.

What are you looking for after graduation?

An entry-level role or internship in embedded software, robotics, or systems engineering where I can keep building hands-on projects.
