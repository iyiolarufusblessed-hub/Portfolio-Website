Hobbies & Interests

Life away from the breadboard

Engineering is most of my week, but these are the things that keep me curious and balanced outside of class.

🤖 Amateur Robotics

Building small robots and automation demos with off-the-shelf microcontrollers — a hands-on extension of what I study in class.

🎨 Designing

Creating visual designs and graphics, from branding concepts to web interfaces.

📷 Photography

Capturing everyday moments and scenes; I enjoy the technical side of exposure and composition as much as the creative side.

🔬 Research

Reading into new technologies and topics that intersect with engineering and design.

🧘 Meditating

Taking time to reflect and reset, which keeps me focused and grounded through demanding coursework.

✈️ Traveling

Exploring new places, cultures, and perspectives whenever I get the chance.

Why It Matters

I've found that hobbies outside of formal coursework often feed back into how I approach engineering problems — design sharpens attention to detail, research keeps me curious about new tools, and meditating helps me stay focused through demanding project work.
