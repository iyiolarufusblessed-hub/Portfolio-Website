Hobbies & Interests

Life away from the breadboard

Engineering is most of my week, but these are the things that keep me curious and balanced outside of class.

🤖 Amateur Robotics

Building small robots and automation demos with off-the-shelf microcontrollers — a hands-on extension of what I study in class.

♟️ Chess

Casual tournament play, with a particular interest in endgame theory and pattern recognition — surprisingly similar to debugging.

📷 Photography

Street and landscape photography with a mirrorless camera; I enjoy the technical side of exposure and composition as much as the creative side.

🌐 Open-Source Contribution

Occasional contributor to small embedded-systems libraries on GitHub — a good way to read other people's code and give back.

Why It Matters

I've found that hobbies outside of formal coursework often feed back into how I approach engineering problems — chess sharpens pattern recognition, photography sharpens attention to detail, and open-source work builds comfort reading unfamiliar codebases.
